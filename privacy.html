Privacy Policy

PCS Wealth

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how PCS Wealth ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application. We are committed to ensuring that your privacy is protected and we respect your right to keep your financial data private.

Information We Collect

PCS Wealth is designed with privacy as a core principle. The app stores all financial data locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store your personal financial information on external servers.

The limited information that may be collected includes:

  • Device Information: Basic device model and operating system version, used solely for compatibility and crash diagnostics.
  • Usage Analytics (Optional): Anonymous, aggregated usage statistics that cannot identify you personally. This data helps us improve the app experience.
  • User-Provided Data: Financial information you choose to enter into the app (transactions, holdings, budgets, goals) is stored only on your device.

How We Use Your Information

Any information collected is used exclusively for:

  • Providing and maintaining core app functionality
  • Improving app performance and user experience
  • Diagnosing and fixing technical issues
  • Responding to your support requests when you contact us

We will never sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data Storage and Security

All financial data you enter into PCS Wealth is stored locally on your device using secure iOS storage mechanisms. Your data is protected by iOS's built-in security features, including device-level encryption.

To protect your data, we recommend:

  • Using a device passcode or biometric authentication (Face ID / Touch ID)
  • Keeping your device's operating system up to date
  • Enabling automatic iOS backups through iCloud (encrypted backups)

Third-Party Services

PCS Wealth does not integrate with third-party advertising networks, social media tracking, or analytics services that profile users. The app is fully self-contained.

Children's Privacy

PCS Wealth is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

Your Rights

You have full control over your data. At any time you can:

  • Delete any individual record (transaction, holding, budget, goal) within the app
  • Export all your data using built-in export functionality
  • Delete the app, which removes all locally stored data from your device
  • Contact us to request information about any data we may hold

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal, operational, or regulatory reasons. We will post the updated policy within the app and on this page, with a revised "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes indicates acceptance of the updated policy.

International Users

PCS Wealth is available worldwide. Regardless of your location, your financial data remains stored on your device and is not transmitted to us or any third party. By using the app, you agree to this Privacy Policy.
