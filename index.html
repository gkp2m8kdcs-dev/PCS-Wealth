About PCS Wealth

PCS Wealth is a powerful and intuitive personal finance application designed to help you take complete control of your financial life. Whether you're tracking daily expenses, managing a diverse investment portfolio, planning for retirement, or working toward specific financial goals, PCS Wealth provides all the tools you need in one beautiful, easy-to-use app.

Built with privacy and simplicity in mind, all your financial data stays securely on your device. No accounts required, no data shared with third parties.

Key Features

📊 Dashboard

Get a complete overview of your net worth, assets, liabilities, and recent transactions at a glance.

💼 Portfolio Tracking

Monitor stocks, mutual funds, crypto, real estate, and other holdings with detailed allocation analysis.

💰 Budget Management

Create custom budgets by category and track your spending against your limits in real time.

📈 Insights & Reports

Analyze your spending patterns, view detailed reports, and gain valuable financial insights.

🎯 Financial Goals

Set and track progress toward savings goals, retirement targets, and major purchase plans.

🧮 Compound Interest Calculator

Visualize the growth of your investments over time with interactive charts and projections.

📝 Transaction History

Record and categorize all your income and expenses with powerful search and filtering.

📄 Document Storage

Securely store receipts, contracts, and important financial documents within the app.
