Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is my financial data secure?
A: All financial data is stored locally on your device using iOS's secure storage. We never transmit your financial data to any external server. For best protection, enable device passcode and Face ID / Touch ID.
Q: Do I need to create an account?
A: No. PCS Wealth works fully offline. No account, email, or registration is required to use the app.
Q: Can I back up my information?
A: Yes. You can export your data through the app's built-in export feature. Additionally, your data is included in your standard iOS encrypted iCloud or iTunes backups if enabled.
Q: How do I delete my data?
A: You can delete individual entries within the app. To remove everything, simply delete the app from your device — all locally stored data will be removed.
Q: Does the app support multiple currencies?
A: PCS Wealth supports multiple currencies. You can configure your preferred currency in the app settings.
Q: Can I import data from other finance apps?
A: Currently, PCS Wealth supports manual entry and CSV import for transactions. We're continuously working to add more import options.
Q: Is the app free?
A: PCS Wealth is free to download and use. All core features are available at no cost.
Q: Which iOS versions are supported?
A: PCS Wealth supports iOS 15.0 and later. We recommend keeping your device updated to the latest iOS version for the best experience.
Q: How do I report a bug or request a feature?
A: Please email us at the address above with details. Include steps to reproduce any issue, your device model, and iOS version.

Troubleshooting Tips

App is running slowly

  • Close and reopen the app
  • Restart your device
  • Make sure you have enough free storage on your device
  • Update to the latest version of PCS Wealth from the App Store

Data not displaying correctly

  • Pull down to refresh the relevant screen
  • Check that you've entered data in the correct format (especially currency amounts)
  • Verify that filters and date ranges are set as expected

Lost data after update

If your data appears to be missing after an update, try restoring from your most recent iOS backup. If the issue persists, please contact support immediately.
